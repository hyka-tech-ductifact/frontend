#### 1. Information We Collect

- -**Personal Data:**
  Your full name and email address.
- -**Account Credentials:**
  Encrypted password hashes (we never store plain-text passwords).
- -**Application & Project Data:**
  Project details, metadata, configurations, and assets created or saved within the app.
- -**Technical Data:**
  Session logs and authentication tokens.

#### 2. How We Use Your Data

- -To identify your account, personalize your profile, and deliver core application services.
- -To save, manage, and display your project data within your workspace.
- -To send security alerts, updates, and account recovery emails.

#### 3. Data Security & Sharing

- -We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or project data to third parties.

- -Project data is isolated and accessible only by you (and authorized team members if sharing features are enabled).

#### 4. Your Data Rights

You can request access to your personal information, request project data exports, or delete your account and all associated project data at any time.
